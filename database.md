## Prepare a Software Requirement Specification (SRS) document for the project

### Architecture & Design

- SDD (Software Design Document): Details code structure and data models.
- SAD (Software Architecture Document): Outlines high-level system structures and patterns.  

### Testing & Quality Assurance
- STP (Software Test Plan): Defines the testing strategy and scope.
- STR (Software Test Report): Logs the actual results of testing.
- SQAP (Software Quality Assurance Plan): Sets quality standards and procedures.
- SQAR (Software Quality Assurance Report): Audits compliance with quality standards.

### Deployment & Maintenance
- SDP (Software Deployment Plan): Maps out the release strategy.
- SDR (Software Deployment Report): Records the outcome of the deployment.
- SMP (Software Maintenance Plan): Details long-term support and bug-fixing procedures.
- SMR (Software Maintenance Report): Tracks post-release updates and fixes.

### Management & Control
- SCMP (Configuration Management Plan): Defines version control and tracking rules.
- SCMR (Configuration Management Report): Audits current baselines and changes.
- SRMP (Risk Management Plan): Identifies potential project threats and mitigation.
